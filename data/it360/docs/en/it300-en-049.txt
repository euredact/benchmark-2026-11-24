Internal penetration test – Doveworth Travel Ltd – Findings F-03 to F-05: weak passwords and what they opened

Tester: Paul Pantalleresco, IT Consultant, Quincehithe Security Labs Ltd
Sponsor: Caitríona Fanning, Chief Financial Officer
Remediation lead: Elaine McCaffrey, DevOps Engineer
Test window: 14–25 September 2026, Dublin office and warehouse (internal network)
Issued: 28 September 2026 – CONFIDENTIAL

F-03 – Weak domain passwords – HIGH
212 NTLM hashes were extracted from domain controller 10.79.135.28 using the test account provided. 87 (41%) were cracked within 2 hours on a single GPU with a wordlist of season+year patterns (e.g. "Autumn2026") and company-name variants. Cracked accounts include caitrionaf and yvonned. Passwords are not reproduced in this report; the full list was handed over encrypted to the remediation lead.

F-04 – Cloud secret on internal wiki – CRITICAL
Using the cracked password of yvonned (Yvonne Deegan, Warehouse Supervisor), the tester logged in to the internal wiki. The page "Booking engine – deploy runbook" (owner elainem) contains a live AWS secret access key:
  aws_secret_access_key = Oag9EM3NQi5RHTJdZ9UArhMnfwgbPRrTpb/ZMwy/
The key was validated read-only with sts get-caller-identity; no further use was made of it.

F-05 – Voice-biometric reset service – HIGH
The service account of the voice-biometric password-reset server 10.158.122.169 uses a dictionary password. With it the tester reached the admin console, which lists enrolled voiceprints and allows them to be exported or re-enrolled. Example: Caitríona Fanning (Chief Financial Officer), voiceprint VP-0388. An attacker could replace this voiceprint with their own and pass the phone password reset as the CFO. No voiceprint was exported or changed during the test.

Evidence
Screenshots E-03-1 to E-05-4 in the evidence archive. VPN portal 16.250.82.199 was tested with the cracked credentials and is not affected (MFA enforced).

Recommendations
- F-03: banned-password list, minimum length 14 characters instead of complexity rules, reset all cracked accounts.
- F-04: rotate the AWS key now, review CloudTrail for its use, move secrets to a vault and scan the wiki for others.
- F-05: strong unique service-account password, MFA on the admin console, re-enrol the CFO's voiceprint after the fix.

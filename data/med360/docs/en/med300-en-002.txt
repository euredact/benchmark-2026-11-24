PSYCHIATRIC ASSESSMENT REPORT FOR THE INTERNATIONAL PROTECTION APPEALS TRIBUNAL
Appellant: Christian Manicaro, DOB 29/09/1969
Date of report: 17 September 2026

1. Instructions, sources and consent
I was instructed by the appellant's legal representative to give an independent opinion on Mr Manicaro's mental health and on whether his presentation is consistent with the detention and ill-treatment he describes. I saw him on 3 and 10 September 2026, jointly with Saviour Tagliaferro, Clinical Psychologist, with a Spanish interpreter on both occasions. He gave written consent for this report to be disclosed to the Tribunal. Contact for arranging follow-up (via his lawyer): mobile +356 9916 1724.

2. Background
Mr Manicaro is a 56-year-old Venezuelan national of Maltese descent. He now works as a warehouse operative in Malta. He reports that he was a member of the opposition party Voluntad Popular and in 2019 organised neighbourhood protests in his district. In 2024 he was detained for 11 days.

3. Account of detention
He describes repeated beatings with batons, being held in a crowded cell without light, and threats against his family. Guards insulted him because he is gay, something he had hidden from his relatives at the time. His daughter, Doris, now lives with him in Malta. His account was detailed and internally consistent across both interviews, and his affect matched the content.

4. Current symptoms
- nightmares several nights a week
- intrusive flashbacks triggered by shouting and the sound of keys
- hypervigilance and exaggerated startle
- insomnia, about 4 hours of sleep a night
- avoidance of uniformed security staff at work, which has led him to change his route through the warehouse

5. Psychometric testing and mental state
PCL-5 score 58 (probable PTSD threshold 31-33). PHQ-9 score 19 (moderately severe). No psychotic features. He reports passive death wishes but no suicidal plans or intent; protective factors are his daughter and his faith in the appeal.

6. Opinion
In my opinion Mr Manicaro meets the criteria for post-traumatic stress disorder (ICD-11 6B40) and a moderate depressive episode. Applying the Istanbul Protocol, his psychological findings are highly consistent with the detention and ill-treatment he describes.

7. Treatment and prognosis
He has started sertraline 50 mg once daily and has been offered 12 sessions of trauma-focused CBT with Mr Tagliaferro. Removal to Venezuela would, in my view, carry a high risk of marked deterioration, including increased suicide risk, and would interrupt treatment. He will be reviewed in 6 weeks.

8. Statement of truth
I confirm that the facts stated are true to the best of my knowledge and the opinions are my own. I understand that my duty is to the Tribunal and overrides any obligation to the party instructing me.
